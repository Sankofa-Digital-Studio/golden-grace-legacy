# Product variants and image mapping

## Catalogue behaviour

The storefront now displays 27 cards instead of 61. Pure Honey, Creamed Honey, Vitality Blend and each infused flavour have size selectors. Selecting a size updates price and vessel. Cart lines retain the selected SKU, size and price. Distinct gift configurations and bee removal services remain separate.

## Pricing decisions

Existing prices are preserved. Specific flavour records take precedence over generic infused prices. Cinnamon 175g remains R95; generic infused 175g remains R85. The broad infused range supplies smaller and larger options for each listed flavour. Existing multi-size records with one price retain their existing pricing; no missing prices were invented.
