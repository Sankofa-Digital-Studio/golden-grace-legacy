# Golden Grace image selection

Nine original photographs were selected from 197 images for the Golden Grace Honey website. The selection prioritises recognisable branding, clear products and authentic everyday settings. The update targets development and preview; production remains unchanged.

## Review

Pass one screened the full collection for relevance and duplication. Pass two matched photographs to homepage, gifting and texture sections. Pass three checked image references and deployment readiness. A photograph cannot establish a floral variety or batch, so variety-specific product cards retain their existing imagery.

## Commercial comparison

Honey House presents raw honey and gift boxes, while Honey Pot combines raw honey with gifting. These examples support using clear product and gift photography. Golden Grace can distinguish itself through its own branded jars and lived-in outdoor settings. This comparison supports the visual direction; it does not establish sales demand or profitability.
